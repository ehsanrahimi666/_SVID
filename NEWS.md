# svid 0.1.0

* First public release.
* Modules: inputs, generation, redundancy reduction, fast screening,
  evaluation, inference, robustness, guided search, mapping, graphics.
* Exact symbolic canonicalisation of rational band expressions and detection
  of monotone-equivalence classes.
* Classification and regression targets.
* Bundled data: `andong` (Sentinel-2A time series, 3 land-cover classes) and
  `asi_indices` (Awesome Spectral Indices catalogue, 280 published indices).
